2. He likes J.K. Rowling because of the sense of humor in her books.

TRANSCRIPT BÀI NGHE (LỜI THOẠI CHI TIẾT & DẪN CHỨNG ĐÁP ÁN)
Task 1: Conversation between Tom and Lan

Tom: Hi Lan! Why do you come to school so early today?

Lan: Hi Tom! I always get up early at 5.30 a.m. (Câu 1: C), do some morning exercise and go to school at 6.30 a.m.

Tom: Wow! It’s a good habit to keep fit but I’m too lazy for it because I often stay up late to watch films (Câu 6: A). I often get up quite late at 6.15 a.m. except today when my dad has to travel on business (Câu 2: B).

Lan: Watching films is also a good hobby! Films help us learn more about different cultures. (Câu 3: C) They also teach us how to behave or react in some special situations. For example, what to do when there is a fire or an earthquake.

Tom: That’s true. But I think I’ll need to arrange my schedule so that I can get up earlier for morning exercise (Câu 4: B). I want to look as great as you!

Lan: Thank you, Tom! Good health surely helps us study better. (Câu 5: A)

Task 2: Reading Books Hobby

My favourite hobby is reading. I enjoy reading a book when I am free. I started to do it when I was four years old (TF1: F - đúng 4 tuổi chứ không phải 'over 4'). The first time I read books, I felt interested, so I kept reading. The teachers always taught me to read the difficult words. I was happy when I read a story with a happy ending. I was thrilled when I read a detective story. I enjoy reading because I like to explore the imaginative world of my favourite author, J.K. Rowling who wrote Harry Potter (TF2: F - vì thế giới tưởng tượng chứ không phải 'sense of humor'). There are a lot of advantages of reading. Reading can make me relaxed and calm. I can also learn new vocabulary items. Then I can further improve my English (TF3: F - đọc sách giúp cải thiện tiếng Anh rất nhiều). Moreover, it can give me an unlimited imagination, so I can write books in the future (TF4: T - có thể viết sách/trở thành nhà văn). I can learn the different cultures of other countries in the world too. I read at least one hour every day (TF5: T - ít nhất 1 giờ mỗi ngày = as minimum). I usually read it at home (TF6: T). I wish I could read different kinds of books because it might be very challenging.
