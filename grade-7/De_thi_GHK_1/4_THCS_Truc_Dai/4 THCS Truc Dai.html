🎧 Track 3 - Health problems in Britain:

Doctors in Britain are worried because British teenagers eat a lot of crisps, sweets, and fast food (Q1: A). Most teenagers do not eat enough fruit or vegetables (Q2: D), and more than 1 million British children are overweight. Some teenagers say that they do not have enough time to eat good food (Q3: A). This can lead to many health problems when they are older. Fast food was born in America (Q4: C) and is popular all over the world. McDonald's, Pizza Hut, Burger King have restaurants all over the globe (Q5: C - Tom and Jerry is not a fast food brand). No one can deny the convenience of fast food. However, eating too many hamburgers, fries, and soda does not nourish the body with vitamins and minerals. Fast food is short of many important nutrients (apples are healthy fruit, not fast food - Q6: B). Now doctors are giving young people books about a good diet. They need to have a healthy diet to feel better and live longer.

🎧 Task 11 - Nam interviews Mark about his hobby:

Nam: What's your hobby, Mark?
Mark: Making cakes. (Statement 1: T)
Nam: Wow, it's a strange hobby for a boy. (Statement 2: F - it is NOT similar to many boys)
When did you start that hobby?
Mark: I got interested in making cakes two years ago after watching different series of American Junior Chef. (Statement 3: T) Then I watched many clips about making cakes on YouTube.
Nam: What kind of cake can you make now?
Mark: I can make many different kinds, like banana bread, butter cakes, apple cakes, and of course birthday cakes. (Statement 4: F - he CAN make birthday cakes)
Nam: How often do you make cakes?
Mark: On special occasions, like birthdays or family gatherings. (Statement 5: T)
Nam: Do you have any plan for your hobby?
Mark: When I finish school, I will open a cake shop. (Statement 6: F - he will open a cake shop, not give it up!)

II Read the following passage and answer the questions (1.6 pts)

Hobbies

When someone does an activity that is not a part of his or her job and is done for fun, it is called a hobby. Most people enjoy hobbies during their free time. Some people spend a lot of time on their hobbies. There are nearly as many kinds of hobbies, as there are people. A hobby can be about making or collecting things, or enjoying an experience.

Creative hobbies are popular. Some creative hobbies are writing, drawing, and making pottery. A lot of people like making jewelry or clothes. People who do these kinds of hobbies sometimes become writers or artists when they retire. Collecting is also popular. Most collectors used to buy coins, stamps, or baseball cards. Today, toys, games, and model cars are trendy. Collectors buy and sell things on the Internet. They also shop online in stores all around the world. Enjoying an experience is a hobby, too. Some people listen to music. Others watch movies or read books. Many people travel or play sports. Helping people is a hobby. There are places you can help build houses for poor families. You can help feed the homeless at soup kitchens. You can also tutor children or help the elderly.

A hobby is something that is done for fun. With so many choices, there is no reason not to enjoy a hobby.
