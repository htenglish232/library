2.4 Điểm

I. Listen to the conversation between Tom and Jack. Circle the best answer A, B or C. You will listen TWICE. (1.2 p)

Mỗi câu đúng được 0.2 điểm

1. What is the interview about?
2. What does Jack like doing in his leisure time?
3. What is Jack's hobby?
4. When did he start his hobby?
5. Which among these that Jack doesn't do with the things he made?
6. What does he say about his hobby?

II. Listen to Tom and Linda talking about their community activities last summer. Decide if the statements are true (T) or false (F). You will listen TWICE. (1.2p)

Mỗi câu đúng được 0.2 điểm

1. Linda and her friends taught English and Maths.
2. Linda and her friends had a lot of fun.
3. Linda and her friends read book to the elderly.
4. Tom and his friends picked up paper and bottles.
5. Tom and his friends cleaned schools.
6. Tom and his friends worked and played together and learnt some skills.
TRANSCRIPT BÀI NGHE (LỜI THOẠI & DẪN CHỨNG ĐÁP ÁN)

Tape Script 1: Tom & Jack (Origami)

Tom: Hi Jack. I'm doing a survey on teenagers' hobbies (Câu 1: A). Can I ask you a few questions?
Jack: Yes, go ahead.
Tom: Thanks. What do you often do in your leisure time?
Jack: Well, it's actually not a popular type of hobbies, but in my free time I like doing origami (Câu 2: C).
Tom: You mean creating things from paper?
Jack: Yes. That's exactly what I do. Folding paper (Câu 3: C).
Tom: How long have you been doing it?
Jack: Well, it all started two years ago on my trip to Japan (Câu 4: B). I watched some origami artists folding some paper animals and I liked it.
Tom: What do you enjoy about it?
Jack: It's easy, inexpensive (= cheap, Câu 6: A) and great to share with others.
Tom: What do you do with the stuff you made?
Jack: I give some to my friends as birthday gifts, and sell some to get money. (Không hề cất trong hộp -> Câu 5: B).

Tape Script 2: Tom & Linda (Community activities)

Tom: So, what did your club do last year?
Linda: We tutored third grade children and helped old people.
Tom: Fantastic! What did you tutor?
Linda: We taught English and maths (Câu TF1: True).
Tom: Awesome! Was it difficult?
Linda: Yes, but we had a lot of fun (Câu TF2: True).
Tom: I see. How did you help the elderly?
Linda: We cooked for them, and did some cleaning too (Không đọc sách -> Câu TF3: False).
Tom: Great! I'm sure they benefited from it.
Linda: Thanks. It made us feel useful. How about you?
Tom: We picked up paper and bottles in a nearby park (Câu TF4: True). We also planted some trees.
Linda: Fabulous! Did you water them too?
Tom: Sure, we watered them very often the first few weeks, and enjoyed watching them grow. (Không hề dọn trường học -> Câu TF5: False).
Linda: Glad to hear it, Tom.
Tom: Yes, it was a really good time. We worked and played together, and we learnt some skills, too (Câu TF6: True).

2.6 Điểm

I. Choose the correct word A, B, C or D for each gap to complete the following passage. (1.0 pt)

Mỗi câu đúng được 0.2 điểm

Passage 1: Mary O'Hara

Mary O’Hara likes to relax in the garden of her home near London. She does not have much time for this (1) ………… she is a very busy woman. She (2) …………… her housework, and cooking like everybody else, and she spends (3) …………… with her friends. She is best known in this country as a folk singer. She has her own popular television show and does concerts all (4) ……… the world.

She is a shy person and is often nervous in front of audiences. But she loves to make people happy with her music. (5) ………… she has not married again, she has plenty of friends and enjoys her busy life.

* Lưu ý giáo viên: Chỗ trống (5) đã được chuẩn hóa vị trí liên từ chính xác trước mệnh đề "she has not married again" để đảm bảo chuẩn cấu trúc câu tiếng Anh.

1. (1) she is a very busy woman
2. (2) She ____________ her housework
3. (3) spends ____________ with her friends
4. (4) does concerts all ________ the world
5. (5) ________ she has not married again, she has plenty of friends
