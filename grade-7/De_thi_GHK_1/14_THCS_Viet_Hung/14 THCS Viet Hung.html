TRANSCRIPT (Lời thoại bài nghe chi tiết)
✦ Audio Script - Task 1 (Hội thoại về sở thích của Trang)

MC: Welcome back to our program. Next, we have Trang from Tran Phu school. Hi Trang!

Trang: Hi!

MC: What is your favourite hobby?

Trang: I love making paper dolls (Câu 1). I do it whenever I have free time.

MC: Oh, when did you start your hobby?

Trang: Three years ago (Câu 2). My aunt came back from Germany, and she gave me a paper doll. I loved it right away.

MC: Do you share this hobby with anyone?

Trang: Yes. My sister (Câu 3) likes it too. We sometimes have the same ideas and make similar paper dolls.

MC: Do you find it difficult?

Trang: A little. Because in order to have unique and beautiful paper dolls, I need to be creative and skillful. (difficult - Câu 4)

MC: I agree. How do you make paper dolls?

Trang: First, I draw dolls on cardboard and cut them out. Then I draw clothes on paper (Câu 5), cut them out and finally put them on the dolls. You know, it is very interesting (Câu 6) to design clothes for my paper dolls.

MC: You can design clothes?

Trang: A little. In order to design more beautiful clothes, I often learn about the clothes that people in different countries wear.

MC: Wow, do you think you will continue your hobby in the future?

Trang: Sure. My mom said I will become a great designer, but I'm not sure. My dream is to become a teacher.

✦ Audio Script - Task 2 (Bí quyết giữ gìn sức khỏe)

Good morning, children! Today I’ll give you some tips on how to keep fit.

First, remember to have different kinds of food (Câu 1: True). Some of you may like hamburgers and want to have it all the time, but that’s unhealthy (Câu 2: False). Change your food as often as you can!

Second, drink more water and milk (Câu 3: False vì đề bảo shouldn't). They will bring you more good minerals and help you build strong bones.

Fruit juice is also good for you (Câu 4: True) but avoid soft drinks because they have lots of added sugar.

The next tip is: Do not use your phones, tablets, computers or watch TV too much! (Câu 5: True). They are not good for your eyes and they keep you away from physical activities.

Always keep your body active! Playing football, badminton, or any kinds of sports will surely bring you good health (Câu 6: True).

II. Read the passage and do the tasks (1.6 pts)

Lan’s grandparents live in the countryside, and they have a big garden. They spend a lot of time working on it. When she visits them in summer, she often joins them in the garden, and they teach her a lot of things about vegetables. Vegetables come in different shapes, sizes, and colors. They also grow differently as well. Carrots and potatoes grow under the ground. Some kinds of beans need a high net to grow. Cabbages and cauliflower grow above the ground. Her grandfather says that coloured vegetables are very good for health because they provide a lot of natural vitamins. White cauliflower is rich in vitamin C. Orange carrots and red tomatoes contain vitamin A. Pumpkins have vitamins A, B, C, and E. Her grandfather also says that gardening is a good way of exercising. It helps him keep fit and strong.

1. What does Lan often do when she visits her grandparents in summer? (0.4 pt)

2. Do vegetables have different colors? (0.4 pt)

3. Why are coloured vegetables very good for health? (0.4 pt)
