Track 21: Camping Activities

Camping can be lots of fun. When we go camping, there is plenty of time for games (1) with friends. If we camp near a beach, we can build sandcastles (2), go swimming, or play beach volleyball. In other places, we can do other activities, like playing football, cycling, bushwalking, listening to music, or drawing. In the evening, we can have dinner by an open (3) fire. It's a good time to enjoy the fresh air, tell stories, and laugh with friends. While we go camping, there is no television or computer (4). But don't worry, you'll have a great time (5). Don't forget to bring important things like food and water / drink (6), a sleeping bag, a compass, and some insect cream.

Track 13: Healthy Habits

Healthy habits help us keep fit and avoid disease (1 -> True). Here is some advice: Eat more fruits and vegetables (2 -> False: đề ghi "less"), especially coloured ones like carrots and tomatoes. They provide a lot of vitamins. Eat meat, eggs, and cheese, but not too much (3 -> False: đề ghi "eat much"). You may put on weight (4 -> True). Drink enough water, but not soft drinks (5 -> False: đề ghi "and soft drinks"). Be active and exercise every day. Do outdoor activities like cycling, swimming, or playing sports. They keep you fit (6 -> True). Go to bed early and get about 8 hours of sleep daily. Keep your room tidy and clean. Open windows to let in fresh air and sunshine.
