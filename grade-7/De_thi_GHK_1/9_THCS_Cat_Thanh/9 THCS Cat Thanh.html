Task 1: Tom and Linda talking about community activities

Tom: So, what did your club do last year? [Dẫn chứng Câu 1 $\rightarrow$ A]

Linda: We tutored 3rd grade children and helped old people.

Tom: Fantastic! What did you tutor?

Linda: We taught English and maths. [Dẫn chứng Câu 2 $\rightarrow$ B]

Tom: Awesome! Was it difficult?

Linda: Yes. But we had a lot of fun.

Tom: I see! How did you help the elderly?

Linda: We cooked for them and did some cleaning, too. [Dẫn chứng Câu 3 $\rightarrow$ B]

Tom: Great! I'm sure they benefited from it.

Linda: Thanks! It made us feel useful. How about you?

Tom: We picked up paper and bottles in a nearby park. [Dẫn chứng Câu 4 $\rightarrow$ B] We also planted some trees. [Dẫn chứng Câu 5 $\rightarrow$ A]

Linda: Fabulous! Did you water them too?

Tom: Sure. We watered them very often the first few weeks and enjoyed watching them grow.

Linda: Glad to hear it, Tom.

Tom: Yes. It was a really good time. We worked and played together, and we learnt some skills, too. [Dẫn chứng Câu 6 $\rightarrow$ B: a happy time]

Task 2: Julia talking about her hobby

Hello, My name is Julia. My favourite pastime is taking photographs. [Dẫn chứng TF1 $\rightarrow$ True]

On my eleventh birthday, my dad gave me a camera and showed me how to take a picture of my birthday cake. [Dẫn chứng TF2: Bố tặng máy ảnh chứ không phải mẹ $\rightarrow$ False]

It was amazing! Since then I have been very keen on doing it. I have to go to school every weekday, so I only have time for my hobby at the weekend. [Dẫn chứng TF3: Chỉ có thời gian cuối tuần chứ không phải mỗi ngày $\rightarrow$ False]

I always get up early on Saturday morning and then I go to the beach to take pictures of beautiful sunrises. [Dẫn chứng TF4 $\rightarrow$ True]

After that, I go to the park to take photos of the flowers there. They look fresh in the morning. [Dẫn chứng TF5 $\rightarrow$ True]

I only show my photos to my parents, not anyone else. [Dẫn chứng TF6: Chỉ cho bố mẹ xem chứ không phải mọi người $\rightarrow$ False]

I hope I will be a famous photographer in the future.
