Audio Script Task 1: Healthy Habits

Healthy habits help us keep fit and avoid disease (Q1 - T). Here is some advice. Eat more fruit and vegetables, especially coloured ones like carrots and tomatoes. They provide a lot of vitamins (Q2 - T). Eat meat, eggs, and cheese, but not too much (Q3 - F: You should eat a lot... is FALSE). You may put on weight. Drink enough water, but not soft drinks (Q5 - F: Drink soft drinks is FALSE). Be active and exercise every day. Do outdoor activities like cycling, swimming, or playing sports. They keep you fit. Go to bed early and get about 8 hours of sleep daily (Q4 - F: five hours is FALSE), so you will not feel tired. Keep your room tidy and clean (Q6 - T). Open windows to let in fresh air and sunshine on fine days.

Audio Script Task 2: John's Routine

John is my best friend. He is a teacher (Q1: B). He is teaching in a school in London. Every morning, he gets up at about 7 o’clock (Q2: B). He has breakfast and drinks a cup of coffee (Q3: C). Then he goes to work. The school is far from his house, so he can’t walk there. He must go by bus (Q4: A). He has lunch at a restaurant nearby (Q5: C). In the afternoon, he works until 5 or 5.30 and then he goes home. He doesn’t work on Saturdays or Sundays (Q6: C - at weekend). He goes to the cinema or theatre. He is married and he has a son. He loves his family. They are very happy.
