TRANSCRIPT (LỜI THOẠI BÀI NGHE CHI TIẾT)

Đã mở khóa sau khi nộp
★ Task 1 - Tom & Linda's Community Activities:

Tom: Linda, what did you and your friends do for community activities last summer?

Linda: We had a busy summer! First, we taught English and maths (Câu 1) to twenty street children. Then, we visited the nursing home and cooked for (Câu 2) the elderly there.

Tom: That’s wonderful! How about your environmental projects?

Tom: Well, my group went to the city park. We picked up paper and bottles (Câu 3) around the lake. We also grew some trees (Câu 4) along the main road.

Linda: Did you take care of them?

Tom: Yes, of course. We watered the trees very often in the first (Câu 5) few weeks and enjoyed watching them grow. We worked and played together, and we learned some great skills (Câu 6)!

★ Task 2 - Nam's Hobbies (Chuẩn từ Hướng dẫn chấm):

"Hello, my name's Nam. I'm twelve years old and I live in Ha Noi. I'm very sporty. I'm the leader of my school football team. My favourite hobby is playing football, of course. Besides, I also like playing basketball (Câu TF1: T). I love watching TV, too (Câu TF4: T) and I am good at playing computer games (Câu TF2: T). My big sister, Ha, is fourteen and her only hobby is reading books (Câu TF3: F). She doesn't like playing any sports (Câu TF6: F). She doesn't like watching TV or playing computer games like me. There are a lot of books in her bedroom (Câu TF5: F - không phải trong phòng của Nam). Sometimes she shares her favourite books with me and we discuss them in the evening."
