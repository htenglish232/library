★ Task 1 - Tom & Linda's Community Activities:

Tom: Linda, what did you and your friends do for community activities last summer?

Linda: We had a busy summer! First, we taught English and maths (Câu 1) to twenty street children. Then, we visited the nursing home and cooked for (Câu 2) the elderly there.

Tom: That’s wonderful! How about your environmental projects?

Tom: Well, my group went to the city park. We picked up paper and bottles (Câu 3) around the lake. We also grew some trees (Câu 4) along the main road.

Linda: Did you take care of them?

Tom: Yes, of course. We watered the trees very often in the first (Câu 5) few weeks and enjoyed watching them grow. We worked and played together, and we learned some great skills (Câu 6)!
