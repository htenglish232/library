MS. LILY'S QUICK REVIEW NOTE (Học nhanh cùng cô)

📌 Vocabulary Essentials:

  • elderly people: người cao tuổi
  • people in need: người có hoàn cảnh khó khăn
  • rubbish / trash: rác thải

💡 Past Simple Rules (Thì Quá khứ đơn):

- Regular verbs: Add -ed (plant → planted, help → helped, pick up → picked up).
- Irregular verbs: (teach → taught).
- Pronunciation of -ed: /ɪd/ after t, d (planted); /t/ after p, k, f, s, ch, sh (helped); /d/ for remaining voiced sounds.
