Phần III Đọc văn bản và trả lời câu hỏi (Q32 - Q37)

Reading Passage:

Eating habits around the world are different. In Viet Nam, people often eat rice, noodles, and soup. Pho and spring rolls are popular dishes. Many people drink green tea or water. Some people eat at home, while others eat in restaurants. Food prices are different in each place. People use words like “some”, “a lot of”, “many”, and “much” when talking about food. Eating habits show the culture of each country.
