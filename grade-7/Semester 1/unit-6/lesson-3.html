II. Cloze Passage

Chọn từ thích hợp điền vào chỗ trống trong đoạn văn bên dưới.

Quoc Hoc – Hue is a (27) ______ school in Viet Nam. It is located in Hue City. The school has many (28) ______ such as classrooms, a library, and a laboratory. There are over 1,000 students. Students (29) ______ many subjects like Maths, English, and Science. They also take part in outdoor activities such as (30) ______ trees and growing vegetables. The school is very (31) ______.

III. Reading Passage Comprehension

Đọc đoạn văn chi tiết và chọn câu trả lời đúng nhất.

My school is a modern school in the city. It has over 50 classrooms and many facilities such as a library, computer room, and science laboratory. Students study many subjects and join outdoor activities in the school garden. They grow vegetables and plant trees every week. Teachers guide students carefully. Students also work in groups and present projects in class. The school is famous for its good learning environment.

Câu 32. What is the best title for the passage?

Câu 33. How many classrooms are there?

Câu 34. What do students do in the school garden?

Câu 35. What does "they" refer to in the text?

Câu 36. What do students NOT do?

Câu 37. What is the school famous for?

Gốc: Where do you study?

💡 TÓM TẮT BÀI HỌC QUAN TRỌNG (MINI REVIEW)

🔑 Từ vựng trọng tâm (Unit 6)

  • 🏫 facilities (trang thiết bị)
  • 📍 located (được định vị, nằm ở)
  • 🌳 outdoor activities (hoạt động ngoài trời)

🛠️ Ngữ pháp cần nhớ

  • 📌 Thì Hiện tại đơn: "The school has..." (Số ít) / "They have..." (Số nhiều).
  • 📌 Cấu trúc bị động: "Subject + is/are located" (được đặt ở/vị trí ở).
