Street painting festivals are becoming popular in many countries. At these festivals, artists draw and paint in public places such as streets, parks, and schools. Some performances include music, dance, and magic shows.

People can watch, take photos, and enjoy the art. Many visitors say these festivals are very different from traditional art exhibitions. They are more exciting and creative. Students often join these events to learn about art and express their ideas.
