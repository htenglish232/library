Part III Read the passage and choose the best answers (Questions 32–37)

Healthy living means having good habits to stay fit and strong. People should eat healthy food like vegetables and fruit. They should avoid fast food and soft drinks.

Exercise regularly helps the body become active and strong. Sleeping early is also important because it helps people feel fresh and not tired.

Bad habits like eating too much sweet food or going to bed late can affect health. Many people today try to change their habits to live longer and healthier lives.

Mini Review - Cấu trúc trọng tâm bài học (Unit 2)

1. Từ vựng cần nhớ (Keywords)

  • healthy living: lối sống lành mạnh
  • avoid fast food: tránh đồ ăn nhanh
  • stay fit: giữ dáng, khỏe mạnh

2. Ngữ pháp trọng tâm (Grammar)

  • Hiện tại đơn (Present Simple): Diễn tả thói quen, chân lý.
    S (số ít) + V-s/es (ví dụ: She avoids fast food).
  • Động từ khuyết thiếu (Should/Avoid):
    should + V-inf | avoid + V-ing (ví dụ: avoid eating sweets).
