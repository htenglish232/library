12 câu / 2.4 đ

I. Listen to the conversation between Nam and Mrs Nhi, his aunt. Choose the best answer A, B, C or D. (1.2 pts)

Em bấm nút "Nghe bài hội thoại" để nghe giọng đọc bản ngữ (Nghe 2 lần).

Audio Transcript 1 (Lời thoại & Dịch nghĩa):

Nam: Hello, Auntie Nhi. How are you?

Aunt Nhi: Hey, Nam. I'm good. And you? How about your new school?

Nam: I'm good, too. Yeah, my new school is new and large. There are fifty-eight classrooms, a large library, and three labs. There is also a swimming pool.

Aunt Nhi: Wow! Interesting!

Nam: Sure. I like our new flat, too. It's not so large with two bedrooms but enough for our family. I like the balcony and the view in front of the building best. It's quiet around there.

Aunt Nhi: Nice to hear! It's near your school so you can walk there, right?

Nam: Yes. Oh, I have a new classmate. Her name is An. She lives in the next block. We walk to school every morning together.

Aunt Nhi: Great! What does she look like?

Nam: Well, she has long black hair. She's tall and slim.

Aunt Nhi: Ok. You must be so happy in a new place with new friends.

Nam: Yeah.

II. Listen to Mai talking about her house. Choose True (T) or False (F). (1.2 pts)

Em bấm nút "Nghe bài đọc Mai" để nghe thông tin về ngôi nhà của Mai.

Audio Transcript 2 (Lời thoại & Dịch nghĩa):

Mai: Hello everyone! My name is Mai. I live in Ha Noi. I live with my parents. There are six rooms in our house: a living room, a kitchen, two bedrooms, and two bathrooms. I love our living room the best because it's bright. It's next to the kitchen. I have my own bedroom. It's small but beautiful. There's a bed, a desk, a chair, and a bookshelf. It also has a big window and a clock on the wall. I often read books in my bedroom.
