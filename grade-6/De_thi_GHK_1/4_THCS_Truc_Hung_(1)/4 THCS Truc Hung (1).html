Bấm nút bên dưới để nghe toàn bộ bài đọc của Task 1

Tapescript Task 1 (Nội dung bài nghe):

"Mai lives near my house. She is my best friend. We have known each other for 2 years. She is tall and pretty. She has long straight hair and a round face. She often likes wearing T-shirts and jeans, not skirts or dresses. She says she feels comfortable with these clothes. My horoscope sign is Pisces, but hers is Libra. She is really friendly and talkative. She is also a creative girl. She often has many new ideas whenever we work or play together. I like her so much because she is a really interesting friend. I am looking forward to this summer. We will have lots of time."

Dịch nghĩa: Mai sống gần nhà tôi. Bạn ấy là bạn thân nhất của tôi. Chúng tôi biết nhau được 2 năm. Mai cao ráo và xinh xắn. Bạn ấy có mái tóc dài thẳng và khuôn mặt tròn. Mai thường thích mặc áo phông và quần bò, không thích mặc váy. Bạn ấy nói cảm thấy thoải mái với trang phục này. Cung hoàng đạo của tôi là Song Ngư, còn của Mai là Thiên Bình. Bạn ấy rất thân thiện và hoạt ngôn. Bạn ấy cũng rất sáng tạo, thường có nhiều ý tưởng mới mỗi khi chúng tôi học hoặc chơi cùng nhau. Tôi quý bạn ấy vô cùng vì bạn ấy là một người bạn thực sự thú vị...

1. Who is the author talking about?

2. What does Mai look like?

3. Which of these sentences describes Mai?

4. Which clothing does Mai like wearing?

5. Which of the signs is Mai's sign?

6. Why does the author like Mai?

Bấm nút bên dưới để nghe cuộc đối thoại về ngôi nhà mới của Nga

Tapescript Task 2 (Cuộc đối thoại giữa người đàn ông và Nga):

Man: I heard that you moved to a house in the country. Do you like your new house, Nga?

Nga: Oh, I love it. It's really big and cute.

Man: Are there many rooms in your new house?

Nga: Yes, there are. There is a living room, a big kitchen, two bedrooms and two bathrooms. One bathroom is between the two bedrooms. The other one is next to the kitchen.

Man: Is there a dining room?

Nga: No, there isn't. We eat in the kitchen because it's quite big. You know, the thing I love most is that there is a big yard and a large pond in front of the house. These are the places where my family likes to relax or fish on weekends.

Man: Sounds great. What's in your bedroom?

Nga: Hmm… My bedroom is big but it doesn't have much furniture. There's just a bed, a bookcase and an armchair. There's a lamp next to the bookcase on the floor. Another lamp is on the table next to the bed. There is a picture of my family on the table, too.

Man: Nga, please come here and give me a hand.

Nga: Oh, my father is calling me. Talk to you later! Bye!

1. Nga’s new house is in the country.
2. Her new house has 5 rooms.
3. There is a bathroom between the two bedrooms.
4. There is a big yard behind her house.
5. Her bedroom doesn’t have much furniture.
6. There is a picture of her family on the wall.
