Nam: I’m good, too. Yeah, my new school is new and large. There are fifty-eight classrooms, a large library, and three labs. There is also a swimming pool.

Aunt: Wow! Interesting!

Nam: Sure. I like our new flat, too. It’s not so large with two bedrooms but enough for our family. I like the balcony and the view in front of our building best. It’s quiet around there.

Aunt: Nice to hear! It’s near your school so you can walk there, right?

Nam: Yes. Oh, I have a new classmate. Her name is An. She lives in the next block. We walk to school every morning together.

Aunt: Great! What does she look like?

Nam: Well, she has long black hair. She’s tall and slim.

Aunt: Ok. You must be so happy in a new place with new friends.
