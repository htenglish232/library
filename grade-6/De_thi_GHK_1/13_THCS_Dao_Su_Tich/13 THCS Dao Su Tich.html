I. Conversation: Nam and Mrs Nhi, his aunt

Circle the best answer A, B, or C. You will listen TWICE. (1.25 pts)

Tapescript Task 1: Nam & Mrs Nhi Lời thoại bài nghe

Nam: Hello, Auntie Nhi. How are you?

Aunt: Hey, Nam. I’m good. And you? How about your new school?

Nam: I’m good, too. Yeah, my new school is new and large. There are fifty-eight classrooms, a large library, and three labs. There is also a swimming pool.

Aunt: Wow! Interesting!

Nam: Sure. I like our new flat, too. It’s not so large with two bedrooms but enough for our family. I like the balcony and the view in front of the building best. It’s quiet around there.

Aunt: Nice to hear! It’s near your school so you can walk there, right?

Nam: Yes. Oh, I have a new classmate. Her name is An. She lives in the next block. We walk to school every morning together.

Aunt: Great! What does she look like?

Nam: Well, she has long black hair. She’s tall and slim.

Aunt: Ok. You must be so happy in a new place with new friends.

Nam: Yeah. I’m so lucky, Aunt Nhi.

1. How many classrooms are there at Nam’s new school?
2. What does Nam like best about his new flat?
3. How does Nam go to school?
4. Who does Nam go to school with?
5. What does An look like?

I love our living room the best because it's bright. It's next to the kitchen. I have my own bedroom. It's small but beautiful. There's a bed, a desk, a chair, and a bookshelf. It also has a big window and a clock on the wall. I often read books in my bedroom."

2. Mai's house has seven rooms.
3. In her bedroom, there's a clock on the wall.
4. She often listens to music in her bedroom.
