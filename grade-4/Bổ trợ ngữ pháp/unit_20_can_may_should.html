📌 GRAMMAR POINT (Kiến thức trọng tâm)

• Trợ động từ đứng trước động từ chính để bổ sung nghĩa: khả năng, cho phép, khuyên nhủ. Sau trợ động từ luôn là động từ nguyên thể.

• Phủ định: thêm not sau trợ động từ (cannot = can't; should not = shouldn't; riêng may not không viết tắt).

Trợ động từ Chức năng Ví dụ mẫu
Can Ability (Khả năng) I can dance. / I can't dance. / Can you dance?
Can / May Permission (Cho phép / Xin phép) You can (may) watch TV. / Can (May) I come in?
Should Advice (Lời khuyên) You should brush your teeth. / You shouldn't watch TV.
A. Write Can for ability and May for permission
1. the birds fly?
2. I drink some milk?
3. you speak English?
4. the frog jump high?
5. I turn off the radio?
6. I use your computer?
7. I borrow your book?
8. the chicken fly?
B. Read and write should or shouldn't + verb
brush watch do bring play see
1. I have a cold. I a doctor.
2. We too much TV.
3. She her teeth.
4. It is rainy. You your umbrella.
5. You the piano at night.
6. You your homework.
C. Look and write (Quan sát tình huống và hoàn thành câu)
1. Can the frog fly? ➔ It can jump.
2. May I borrow your book? ➔ Here you are.
3. Can I open the window? ➔ It is hot.
4. May I sit down? (Ghế vừa mới sơn) ➔ It is wet.
5. Your room is messy. You clean your room.
6. Everyone is sleeping. You play the piano.
D. Read and unscramble (Sắp xếp từ thành câu đúng)
E. Read and correct (Sửa lỗi sai trong câu)
1. The frog can't flies. ➔
2. May I borrows your eraser? ➔
3. Can I drinks some water? ➔
4. No, you mayn't. ➔
5. You should brushes your teeth. ➔
6. You not should watch TV. ➔
