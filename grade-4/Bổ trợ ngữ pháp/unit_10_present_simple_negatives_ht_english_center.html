💡 GRAMMAR POINT: Câu phủ định thì Hiện tại đơn

  • Dùng khi nói về sự thật hiển nhiên, thói quen hoặc hành động lặp đi lặp lại.
  • Với I, You, We, They hoặc danh từ số nhiều: dùng don't (= do not) + động từ nguyên thể.
  • Với He, She, It hoặc danh từ số ít: dùng doesn't (= does not) + động từ nguyên thể.
Chủ ngữ Trợ động từ phủ định Động từ (Nguyên thể)
I / You / We / They don't drink, wash, study, have, go
He / She / It doesn't drink, wash, study, have, go
A Read and write don't or doesn't.
1. I play. ➔ I don't play. Ví dụ
2. He eats. ➔ He eat.
3. You cry. ➔ You cry.
4. She watches. ➔ She watch.
5. It runs. ➔ It run.
6. We study. ➔ We study.
7. They go. ➔ They go.
8. It flies. ➔ It fly.
B Look and write.
1. (play) She plays the piano. / She doesn't play the flute. Ví dụ
2. (drink)
He milk.
He coffee.
3. (go)
You to an art class.
You to the park.
4. (have)
It a fish.
It a soccer ball.
5. (wash)
They their faces.
They their feet.
6. (study)
I Chinese.
I English.
C Read and write.
1. He / not / fly a kite Ví dụ
➔ He doesn't fly a kite.
2. She / not / ride a bike
3. My parents / not / watch TV
4. The cat / not / sleep
5. You / not / play tennis
D Read and unscramble.
Sắp xếp các từ thành câu hoàn chỉnh.
1. study / They / don't / French Ví dụ
➔ They don't study French.
E Read and correct.
1. I doesn't read comic books. Ví dụ
➔ I don't read comic books.
2. You don't listens to music.
3. We doesn't play soccer.
4. Lisa don't have a brother.
5. We don't goes to the park.
6. The giraffe don't eat carrots.
