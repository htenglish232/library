Unit 15: Future: Be Going To

Thì tương lai gần: Câu khẳng định (Affirmative) & Câu phủ định (Negative)

📖 Cấu trúc trọng tâm (Grammar Point)

• Khẳng định (Affirmative): S + am / is / are + going to + V (nguyên thể) (Dự định sẽ làm gì).

• Phủ định (Negative): S + am / is / are + not + going to + V (nguyên thể) (Dự định sẽ không làm gì).

Chủ ngữ Affirmative (Khẳng định) Negative (Phủ định)
I am going to + V am not going to + V
He / She / It is going to + V is not (isn't) going to + V
We / You / They are going to + V are not (aren't) going to + V
