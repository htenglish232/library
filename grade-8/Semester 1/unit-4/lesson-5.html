III. Read the following passage and answer the questions. (Questions 32–37)

A project about ethnic groups is a good way for students to review Unit 4. First, students choose an ethnic group they are interested in. Then they do a little research about its living place, economy and traditional culture. They can use books, websites, documentaries or interviews with older people.

For example, a group may choose the Thai people. The Thai often live in stilt houses in mountainous areas. Their houses usually stand on high posts and have staircases. They have rich traditional culture with folk dances, folk songs and beautiful costumes. Some Thai families earn their living from growing crops, raising livestock and weaving.

When students make a poster, they should not write too much information. A good poster needs clear headings, short sentences and suitable pictures. After that, students present their poster to the class. Through the project, they learn new words, practise speaking and understand more about the cultural diversity of Viet Nam.

MINI REVIEW - KIẾN THỨC CẦN NHỚ

Ôn tập nhanh các từ vựng & cấu trúc cốt lõi trong bài học Unit 4:

1. Important Vocabulary

  • • stilt house (n): nhà sàn
  • • communal house (n): nhà rông / nhà cộng đồng
  • • ethnic group (n): dân tộc
  • • sticky rice (n): gạo nếp

2. Core Grammar Patterns

  • • Countable / Uncountable: many + N(đếm được số nhiều), much + N(không đếm được).
  • • Purpose Infinitive: use something to do something (dùng cái gì để làm gì).
  • • Past Simple Question: Did + S + V(nguyên thể)?
