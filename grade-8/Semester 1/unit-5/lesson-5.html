III. Read the passage and answer questions 32–37. (1.4 points)

Local customs and traditions are important because they show the culture of a community. In many places, people practise customs during festivals, family events or worshipping ceremonies. For example, some villagers gather at a village temple to prepare offerings and worship their ancestors. Others take part in local festivals, cheer boat racers or enjoy local food.

Children can learn a lot from these activities. When parents take children to local festivals, children can discover the culture of their community. They can see how people celebrate, what food they prepare and why the tradition is meaningful. Storytelling is also a great way to keep local traditions alive. Through stories from grandparents, children learn about old customs, local heroes and family values.

In modern life, some people break with tradition. For example, they may write emails instead of letters or go to a restaurant instead of eating at home. However, many customs are still useful. They help people connect with their families and communities. They also develop children's understanding of local traditions.

MINI REVIEW - CỦNG CỐ KIẾN THỨC

Key Vocabulary (Từ vựng quan trọng):

  • Family reunion: Cuộc sum họp gia đình
  • Prepare offerings: Sắm sửa lễ vật cúng
  • Worship ancestors: Thờ cúng tổ tiên
  • Break with tradition: Phá vỡ truyền thống

Grammar Structures (Cấu trúc ngữ pháp):

  • have a tradition of + V-ing: Có truyền thống làm gì
  • instead of + V-ing: Thay vì làm gì
  • It is a custom for sb + to V: Phong tục cho ai làm gì
  • a way to V: Cách để làm gì
