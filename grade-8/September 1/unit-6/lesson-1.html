II. Read Passage 1 and choose the best answers (Questions 27–31)

People around the world have different lifestyles. In many countries, people eat breakfast at home, but in some places, they often go out for breakfast. Street food is very popular in Asia, while restaurant food is common in Western countries. Many adults are in the habit of buying food on the roadside because it is quick and cheap.

Online learning is also becoming more popular. Students use computers and learn through the internet. If students study online, they will probably learn faster. However, they should also have a balanced lifestyle with healthy food and exercise.

III. Read Passage 2 and answer the questions (Questions 32–37)

Lifestyle differences are common between countries. In Nam’s country, people usually eat breakfast at home and buy food on the roadside. Many adults are in the habit of eating street food. In Tom’s country, people often eat in restaurants and use more modern services.

Nowadays, online learning is becoming popular. Students learn through computers and the internet. If students use technology, they will have more learning opportunities. However, experts say people should not only study online but also spend time with family and friends.

Lifestyle differences also include greetings. Some countries shake hands, while others bow or even press noses together. These differences make the world more interesting and diverse.

Key Grammar Patterns Structures

1. Habit structure: be in the habit of + V-ing (Có thói quen làm gì)
2. First Conditional: If + S + V(hiện tại đơn), S + will + V(bare)
3. Comparative of Adjectives: S1 + be + more + adj + than + S2
