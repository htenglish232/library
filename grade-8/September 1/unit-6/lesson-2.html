II. Read the passage and choose A, B, C or D. (1.0 pt)

Many ethnic groups in Viet Nam live in remote areas. They still keep traditional lifestyles. People often make crafts such as weaving and wood carving. These crafts are very useful and show their native culture.
In the future, people will probably use technology more, but traditional crafts will not disappear. If young people learn these skills, they will help keep culture alive. Tourists like visiting these villages because they can discover unique traditions.

III. Read the passage and choose the correct answer for questions 32–37. (1.4 pts)

Lifestyle and culture are changing in many countries. In the past, people made everything by hand, such as clothes, tools and food. Today, many people use machines and technology. However, in remote areas, traditional lifestyles still exist.

Many ethnic groups practise weaving, making crafts and performing tribal dances. These activities are part of their culture. Tourists often visit these areas to learn about native traditions.

In the future, people will use more technology. They will learn online and communicate through computers. If people do not keep traditions, some cultural values may disappear. Therefore, many communities try to preserve their customs and teach young people.
