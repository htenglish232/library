III. Read the following passage and answer questions 32–37

Viet Nam is home to many ethnic groups, and each group has its own traditional culture. In the Central Highlands, some groups such as the Ba Na and Jrai live in villages with a special communal house. The Rong house is often the tallest and most important building in the village. It is a place where people meet, discuss village work and celebrate important events.

The people in these villages have many traditional activities. Some families grow crops such as rice and corn. Others raise livestock, including cows, pigs and chickens. In some areas, villagers also make wooden statues or play gongs during festivals. The sound of gongs is an important part of their cultural life.

Today, many students learn about ethnic groups through documentaries, books and school projects. These activities help them understand the unique features of each group. They also learn that protecting traditional culture is important. When young people respect different customs, they can help make Viet Nam’s culture richer and stronger.

MINI REVIEW & CORE KNOWLEDGE

Tóm tắt từ vựng & cấu trúc ngữ pháp trọng tâm Unit 4 Tiếng Anh 8

Từ vựng quan trọng (Key Vocabulary)

  • communal house /kəˈmjuːnl haʊs/ (n): nhà cộng đồng, nhà rông
  • stilt house /stɪlt haʊs/ (n): nhà sàn
  • terraced fields /ˈterəst fiːldz/ (n): ruộng bậc thang
  • raise livestock /reɪz ˈlaɪvstɒk/ (v): chăn nuôi gia súc
  • weave scarves /wiːv skɑːvz/ (v): dệt khăn

Ngữ pháp trọng tâm (Grammar Focus)

1. Countable vs. Uncountable Quantifiers:

• Many / a few + danh từ đếm được số nhiều: many ethnic groups, a few wooden statues.

• Much / a little + danh từ không đếm được: much land, a little information.

2. Questions Words & Auxiliaries:

• When (Khi nào) dùng hỏi thời gian: "When does the festival take place?"

• Do/Does + S + V(bare): "Do the women weave clothing?" / "Does your family raise livestock?"
