III. Read the passage and answer the questions (Questions 32–37):

Ok Om Bok Festival is an important festival of the Khmer people in Viet Nam. It is also called the Moon Worshipping Festival. The festival takes place in mid-October of the lunar calendar. It is held to thank the Moon God and mark the end of the Khmer year.

During the Moon God Offering ceremony, people offer young rice and fruits. They believe that the Moon God gives them good crops and protects their lives. After the ceremony, people enjoy many activities such as singing, dancing, games and traditional dances. One of the most exciting events is the Ngo Boat Race. Many teams row long boats on the river, and large crowds cheer for them.

Tourists can enjoy the festival, but they should follow some rules. They should dress up for the ceremony and keep quiet when the monks and elders are talking. It is a good idea to show respect to local people. Visitors shouldn’t climb on animal statues or litter the temple ground. These simple rules help everyone enjoy the festival politely and safely.

MINI REVIEW - KIẾN THỨC CẦN GHI NHỚ
1. Từ vựng trọng tâm (Vocabulary)
  • traditional /trəˈdɪʃənl/ (adj): thuộc truyền thống
  • offer /ˈɒfə/ (v): dâng, cúng, biếu
  • respect /rɪˈspekt/ (n/v): sự kính trọng, tôn trọng
  • maintain /meɪnˈteɪn/ (v): duy trì, bảo tồn
2. Ngữ pháp quan trọng (Grammar)
  • Look forward to + V-ing: Mong chờ, mong đợi làm gì (Ví dụ: look forward to joining).
  • Modals (should / shouldn't + V-bare): Cho lời khuyên (Ví dụ: should dress politely, shouldn't litter).
  • Help somebody (to) V-bare: Giúp ai làm gì (Ví dụ: help us maintain traditions).
