📝 Audio Transcript 1 (Sarah's Job):

Sarah: It's great! I love teaching and I really enjoy working with the children in my class, but it's hard work too.

Man: Do you start early in the morning?

Sarah: Yes, school starts at 9:00, but I always get there at half past 8. Then I have lessons all morning.

Man: Do you get a lunch break?

Sarah: Yes, I usually bring something with me from home, or sometimes I get a sandwich from the school canteen. Then I have a few more lessons in the afternoon, and school finishes at 4:00.

Man: Oh, that's good. You have your evenings free?

Sarah: Not really. I often have work to do.

Man: So you don't have much free time then?

Sarah: Well, I always go to the gym after work for about an hour. When I get home, I just finish my work and then watch TV or read a book.

Man: Do you go out during the week?

Sarah: Very rarely, because I like to go to bed early.

Man: I see. Well, at least you never work at weekends.

Sarah: That's true!

Man: Let's go to the cinema on Saturday night then.

Sarah: What a great idea!
