Đoạn văn hoàn chỉnh:



                                                                                                  Trang 1
Families play an important role in shaping individual behavior (1) from early life. Parents provide emotional
support (2), discipline, and guidance. A family acts as a support (3) system during difficult times, offering
financial and emotional help.

However, modern lifestyles have reduced family relationships (4), which may lead to communication problems
and emotional distance (5). Maintaining work-life balance (6) is therefore essential.

Families also preserve cultural traditions (7) through shared activities. Elderly members contribute wisdom
(8), while younger generations provide innovation and energy (9). Despite differences, family conflicts (10)
can be resolved through empathy and communication.

Lưu ý về đề gốc: Câu 2 và 3 đều cần “support”, trong khi Word Bank chỉ liệt kê một lần. Cụm “reduced family
relationships” (câu 4) chưa tự nhiên; nên sửa thành “weakened family relationships” hoặc “reduced family
interaction”. Câu 7 dùng “cultural traditions” phù hợp với Word Bank, dù bài đọc sử dụng các diễn đạt khác.


PHẦN 3. TRANSLATION (VIETNAMESE → ENGLISH)
Bài dịch tham khảo:

Families play a crucial role in shaping individuals' behavior and values. Parents provide emotional support and
guidance to their children. In modern society, maintaining a work-life balance is essential for preserving
healthy family relationships. Conflicts are inevitable, but they can be resolved through empathy and effective
communication.
